# AI Retail & Commerce Hub

> Merchandising, pricing and personalization that learns.

AI commerce hub — demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, personalization and inventory intelligence. Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — 550+ interlinked AI & IT tools by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

**Live app:** https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-retail-commerce-hub/

## Features
- Demand forecasting at SKU/store/channel level
- Dynamic pricing and promotion optimization
- Product recommendations and search personalization
- Inventory rebalancing and markdown timing
- Customer segmentation and churn prediction
- Integrations: Shopify, Magento/Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce

## Use Cases
- Lift conversion with 1:1 personalization
- Reduce stockouts and overstock simultaneously
- Optimize promotions with margin-aware AI
